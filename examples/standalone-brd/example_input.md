# TaskFlow — Input Brief

## Product Name
TaskFlow

## Initiative
Productivity Suite

## Feature / Idea Summary
TaskFlow — a team task management tool that replaces scattered spreadsheets
and chat-based task tracking with a structured workflow engine. Teams create,
assign, prioritize, and track tasks through configurable statuses with
real-time updates and automated notifications.

## Goals
Reduce average task completion time by 25% within 6 months of launch.
Achieve 80% weekly active usage among onboarded teams within 90 days.
Eliminate duplicate task tracking across tools (target: zero spreadsheet
shadow trackers per team).

## Target Users
Primary: engineering and product teams (5-25 people) at mid-market SaaS
companies. They currently use a mix of Jira, Notion, Slack threads, and
spreadsheets to track work. Secondary: operations teams managing
cross-functional projects with external stakeholders.

## Timing
MVP launch targeting Q3 2026. Beta with 3 internal teams by end of Q2 2026.
GA release with external customers by September 2026.

## Success Metrics
Task completion rate > 85% (vs. current ~60% in spreadsheet trackers).
Time from task creation to first status update < 4 hours.
NPS > 40 within 6 months of GA.

## Known Constraints
AWS-only infrastructure. Must integrate with Slack and GitHub. SSO via
SAML 2.0 required for enterprise tier. Budget ceiling of $15K/month
for infrastructure at launch scale (500 teams, ~5000 users).

## Business Context
Current internal task tracking uses Jira with 45% adoption and a backlog
of 2,300 stale tickets. Teams report spending 3.2 hours/week on status
update meetings that could be replaced by async task updates. Support
ticket volume related to "where is my task?" queries: 120/month.
