# Product Input Brief

## Product Name

Analytics Dashboard

## Initiative

Commerce Platform

## Feature / Idea Summary

A self-service analytics dashboard for e-commerce merchants that shows
real-time conversion funnel data, replacing the current static weekly
PDF reports.

## Goals

Reduce merchant support tickets about reporting by 60%. Increase merchant
retention by 10% within 6 months of launch.

## Target Users

Mid-market e-commerce merchants (50-500 employees) who sell physical goods
online, primarily using Shopify or WooCommerce. Most are not technical —
they rely on their ops team to interpret data.

## Timing

Development start Q3 2026, beta launch Q4 2026.

## Success Metrics

Dashboard adoption rate of 70% within 90 days. Support ticket volume for
reporting drops from 200/month to under 80/month.

## Known Constraints

Must integrate with existing Shopify and WooCommerce APIs. No budget for
third-party BI tools — must be built in-house. Data must stay in our AWS
environment (SOC 2 requirement).

## Business Context

Current merchant dashboard adoption: 23% of active merchants view the weekly
PDF report. Monthly support tickets about reporting: ~200/month. Merchant
churn rate: 4.2%/month (industry benchmark: 3.1%). Average time from merchant
signup to first report view: 11 days. No self-service filtering. Merchants
must email support to get SKU-level data. Shopify integration covers 68%
of merchant base; WooCommerce covers 27%.
