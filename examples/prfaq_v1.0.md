# PRFAQ: Self-Service Conversion Analytics for Merchants

## Press Release

### FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**New Self-Service Analytics Dashboard Gives E-Commerce Merchants Real-Time Conversion Data Without Waiting on Support**

SEATTLE, October 2026. Today we launched a new in-app analytics dashboard that lets mid-market e-commerce merchants see their conversion funnel data in real time. Merchants can now answer their own reporting questions in seconds instead of waiting up to 6 days for the weekly PDF report or filing a support ticket.

The dashboard shows funnel performance from product view to checkout completion, broken down by SKU, traffic source, and device. Data refreshes every 5 minutes. Merchants can drill into any step of the funnel and export the underlying rows.

"I used to email support every week to ask about Tuesday's traffic spike," said one beta merchant. "Now I just open the dashboard and look. It saves me an hour a week and I trust the numbers more because I can see how they were calculated."

The dashboard is included at no additional cost for all merchants on the Growth and Plus plans. It is available today.

## FAQ

### What problem does this solve?

Mid-market merchants get their conversion data in a static weekly PDF. By the time the PDF arrives, the data is up to 6 days old. If a merchant has a question about it, they file a support ticket and wait 1-2 days for an answer. Last quarter, support handled 200 of these tickets a month. The dashboard removes the wait and the ticket.

### Who is the target customer?

Mid-market e-commerce merchants on Shopify or WooCommerce, 50-500 employees, selling physical goods. They typically have one operations or marketing person who looks at reporting and is not a data analyst.

### What does the dashboard show?

Conversion funnel from product view through checkout completion, with drill-downs by SKU, traffic source, device, and date range. It shows trend lines, period-over-period comparisons, and highlights anomalies (a 20%+ change from the prior period).

### How fresh is the data?

Five minutes from event to dashboard. The previous PDF report was 6 days stale on average.

### Why now?

Three things changed. First, support ticket volume for reporting questions grew from 154/month to 200/month over the last year and is on track to hit 240/month by year-end. Second, our Q1 2026 customer interviews showed real-time funnel visibility is the #1 or #2 unmet need for 73% of merchants. Third, our data infrastructure can now support 5-minute refresh at the merchant tier we're targeting (it could not 18 months ago).

### How is this different from Triple Whale or Glew?

Triple Whale is built for performance marketers running paid attribution. It has more features than our target merchants need and the interface assumes a data analyst on staff. Glew is closer in spirit but refreshes data once a day and does not let merchants drill into individual SKU funnels. Our dashboard targets the merchant who wants to see funnel data, drill into a SKU, and get back to running their store.

### What is the success metric?

Two metrics. (1) Adoption: 70% of eligible merchants use the dashboard at least weekly within 90 days of launch. (2) Support deflection: reporting tickets drop from 200/month to under 80/month within 6 months.

### What does it cost?

Included in the Growth and Plus plans at no additional charge. We considered charging for it and decided against it, because the support deflection alone pays for the build.

### What are the biggest risks?

The 73% "unmet need" figure comes from a 12-merchant sample. We may be overestimating demand. We are running a 50-merchant validation survey in May 2026 before committing engineering resources beyond the prototype.

We have not validated this with BigCommerce merchants (~9% of our base). The first version will not support BigCommerce.

### What is explicitly out of scope?

Paid attribution modeling. LTV cohort analysis. Email marketing analytics. Custom report builder. These are all features competitors offer and may be the right next bets, but they are not in v1.
